Quality & Policy

Consistency, 100% material traceability and certified testing guide everything we manufacture & export.

Our Commitment

Quality Assurance Policy

VINIXX Metal & Alloys is committed to supplying products that consistently meet customer and applicable statutory requirements, through a documented quality management system and a culture of continuous metallurgical precision.

01

Raw Material Verification

Every incoming batch is checked with Positive Material Identification (PMI) and mill certificates before processing.

02

In-Process Inspection

Dimensional, roundness, wall-thickness and visual checks are carried out at every fabrication stage.

03

Final Testing & Certification

Hydrostatic testing, ultrasonic examination and chemical composition testing before every dispatch.

04

Customer Feedback & Traceability

Complete batch traceability mapped to Mill Test Certificates EN 10204 3.1 with barcode marking.

100% Inspected Batches
Testing Infrastructure

In-House Testing & Inspection Lab

Our quality laboratory at the Ahmedabad manufacturing plant is equipped with state-of-the-art testing machinery to conduct rigorous mechanical and metallurgical verification according to ASTM, ASME, DIN, and EN specifications.

  • Spectro & PMI Testing: Verifies complete chemical analysis and alloy elemental percentage on-site.
  • Hydrostatic & Pneumatic Test: Up to 10,000 PSI to verify zero pinhole defects and pressure resistance.
  • Non-Destructive Testing (NDT): Ultrasonic, Radiography, Magnetic Particle & Dye Penetrant tests.
  • Mechanical & Metallurgical: Tensile strength, yield, elongation, hardness (Rockwell/Brinell), and IGC practice test.
Our Process

From Raw Material to Dispatch

1. Certified Sourcing

Raw material procured exclusively from prime certified mills with verified chemical composition and heat numbers.

2. Precision Manufacturing

Cutting, forming, heat-treatment and beveling carried out under ISO 9001:2015 controlled process parameters.

3. Rigorous Multi-Stage Testing

Mechanical, hydrostatic, ultrasonic and non-destructive testing performed in-house.

4. Certification & Traceability

Mill Test Certificates (EN 10204 3.1) and Third Party Inspection (TPI: TUV, BV, DNV, SGS, Lloyd's) issued.

5. Export-Grade Packaging & Dispatch

Wooden crates, plastic end-caps, seaworthy shrink-wrapping with tracked dispatch from Ahmedabad.
