Our stainless steel pipes and tubes are manufactured using premium raw material sourced from certified mills, ensuring consistent chemical composition and mechanical strength. Each batch undergoes hydrostatic testing, ultrasonic testing and dimensional inspection before dispatch. We stock a wide inventory across common sizes for immediate delivery, and manufacture custom lengths and finishes on request for large projects.

Whether you need small quantities for a fabrication job or bulk supply for an EPC project, our team ensures documentation, packaging and logistics are handled end-to-end.

  • Grades: SS 304, 304L, 316, 316L, 310, 321
  • Standards: ASTM A312, A213, A358, ASME B36.19
  • Size range: 1/2" to 24" NB
  • Schedule: 5S, 10S, 40S, 80S
  • Testing: Hydro test, UT, PMI, Eddy current
  • Certification: Mill Test Certificate (EN 10204 3.1)
