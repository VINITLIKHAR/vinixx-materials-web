Welcome to VINIXX

Precision-engineered metal, delivered on time, every time

Headquartered in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, VINIXX Metal & Alloys is a leading manufacturer, stockist and exporter of a wide range of high-quality materials including Stainless Steel, Carbon Steel, Alloy Steel, Duplex Steel, Copper, Brass and Aluminium. Every batch that leaves our facility is tested, certified and traceable — because in heavy industry, tolerances matter.

  • ISO 9001:2015 certified manufacturing & quality process
  • In-house testing lab for chemical & mechanical properties
  • Pan-India & export logistics with fast dispatch from Ahmedabad
