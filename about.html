Who We Are

A metal partner built for heavy industry

Founded in 2005, VINIXX Metal & Alloys began as a stockist of carbon steel round bars and has since grown into a full-range manufacturer and exporter of ferrous and non-ferrous metal products. Our modern facility in Ahmedabad, Gujarat serves refineries, EPC contractors, fabricators and OEMs across India and 18+ export markets.

Every product that carries our name goes through documented quality checks — from raw material inspection to final dimensional testing — because reliability is not negotiable in the industries we serve.

  • Manufacturer, stockist & exporter under one roof
  • Serving 1200+ clients across 18+ countries
  • ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management

Our Growth

Milestones Along the Way

2005 — The Beginning

Started as a stockist of carbon steel round bars serving industrial fabricators in Gujarat and Western India.

2011 — Manufacturing Begins

Set up our own pipe & fittings manufacturing unit and obtained ISO 9001 certification.

2016 — Export Expansion

Began exporting to the Middle East, Africa and Southeast Asia.

2022 — Product Diversification

Added copper, brass and aluminium product lines to serve non-ferrous demand.

2026 — Today

Serving 1200+ clients across 18+ countries with a full range of ferrous & non-ferrous products.
